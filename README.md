# Hi, I'm Agustín! 🎮✨

I am a **Game Developer & Technical Artist** based in Mendoza, Argentina, specialized in bridging the gap between complex code and visual assets. My expertise ranges from programming advanced gameplay systems, game logic, and AI in Unreal Engine and Unity, to creating, modeling, and technically integrating production-ready 2D and 3D art.

I focus on building scalable systems, fluid gameplay mechanics, modular UI, and optimizing development pipelines to deliver polished gameplay experiences.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Expertise

### 🚀 Game Programming
* **Unreal Engine (C++ & Blueprints):** Architecture of advanced AI systems (Behavior Trees/State Machines), gameplay mechanics, modular UI systems, landscape design, and animation rigging/blueprints.
* **Unity (C#):** Full-cycle gameplay programming, multi-agent AI implementation, custom UI development, and game architecture.

### 🎨 Art & Asset Pipelines
* **2D Art & Vector Illustration:** High-fidelity digital painting and concept art in Paint Tool SAI 2, alongside precise vector asset creation in Adobe Illustrator.
* **Pixel Art & Animation:** Comprehensive sprite sheet creation, character animation, and seamless tileset design using Aseprite and Adobe Animate.
* **3D Environment & Low-Poly Modeling:** Hard-surface and stylized 3D asset modeling in Blender and specialized retro/grid-based modeling in Crocotile3D.
